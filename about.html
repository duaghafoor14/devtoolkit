What is Dev Tool Kit?

Dev Tool Kit brings a collection of useful developer and file utilities together in one place. Instead of installing different applications or searching for separate tools, you can use practical browser-based utilities whenever you need them.

The website is designed for developers, students, freelancers and anyone who works with data, files, text or everyday development tasks.

Our goal is simple: provide useful tools with a clean interface, straightforward controls and no unnecessary complexity.

Your data and privacy

Many Dev Tool Kit utilities are designed to process data directly inside your browser. This means that supported tasks can be completed locally on your device without requiring an account or uploading your input to a server.

However, you should always avoid entering passwords, private keys, confidential documents or other sensitive information into online tools unless you understand how that particular tool handles your data.

For more information about how the website handles information, please see our Privacy Policy .
