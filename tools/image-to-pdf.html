FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which image formats are supported? +

The tool supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP images.

Are my images uploaded to a server? +

No. Images are processed directly inside your browser and are not uploaded to a server.

Can I change the order of the images? +

Yes. Use the up and down buttons beside each image to change its position before creating the PDF.

Will the image proportions be preserved? +

Yes. Images are fitted onto the PDF page while preserving their original proportions.

Can I use this tool on my phone? +

Yes. The tool is designed to work on both desktop and mobile browsers.
