Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Dev Tool Kit handles information when you use our website and browser-based tools.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Dev Tool Kit is a collection of free browser-based tools designed for developers, students, and anyone who needs simple utilities for working with text, code, files, and other digital content.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep the use of our tools as simple and private as possible. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit and use Dev Tool Kit.

2. Information Processed by Our Tools

Many of the tools on Dev Tool Kit process the information you provide directly inside your web browser.

Depending on the tool, this may include JSON, YAML, CSV, text, regular expressions, JWTs, ZIP files, PDF files, images, or other content that you choose to process.

When a tool is designed for local browser processing, your files and entered content are processed on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to a Dev Tool Kit server.

3. Files You Upload to the Tools

Dev Tool Kit currently provides browser-based file tools such as ZIP Opener, PDF Merge & Split, and Image to PDF.

Files selected for these tools are processed locally in your browser using JavaScript and the required browser-compatible libraries.

Dev Tool Kit does not intentionally store copies of these files on its servers as part of the normal operation of these tools.

However, you should avoid using any online service with highly confidential information unless you are comfortable with the associated risks.

4. Information You Enter

Some tools allow you to enter text, code, patterns, or other information directly into the page.

This information is processed within your browser for the relevant tool's functionality. Dev Tool Kit does not intentionally collect or store the content you enter into these tools as part of their normal operation.

5. Local Browser Storage

Dev Tool Kit uses browser storage for limited website functionality and preferences.

For example, your light or dark theme preference may be stored in your browser using local storage so that the website can remember your selected appearance when you return.

This information is stored on your device and is not used to store the files or content processed by the tools.

6. Cookies

The current Dev Tool Kit tools do not require cookies to process the files or text that you provide to them.

If third-party services are added to the website in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

7. Advertising

Dev Tool Kit may display third-party advertisements in the future, including advertisements provided through services such as Google AdSense.

If advertising services are enabled, those services may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies to provide, measure, or personalize advertisements, subject to their applicable policies and settings.

This Privacy Policy will be updated when necessary to accurately describe any advertising services that are enabled on the website.

8. Third-Party Services

Dev Tool Kit may use third-party services for website hosting, security, performance, analytics, advertising, or other technical purposes.

When such services are used, the relevant third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Dev Tool Kit does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

9. Links to Other Websites

Dev Tool Kit may contain links to external websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites. You should review the privacy policy of any external website you choose to visit.

10. Children's Privacy

Dev Tool Kit is a general-purpose website and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through our tools.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to operate and protect the website.

Because Dev Tool Kit operates over the internet, however, no website, browser, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for deciding whether information is appropriate to process using an online browser-based tool.

12. Your Choices

You can stop using Dev Tool Kit at any time.

You can also clear locally stored website data through your browser settings. This may remove preferences such as your saved theme selection.

If third-party advertising or other services are enabled, additional privacy and advertising controls may be available through those services.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Dev Tool Kit may update this Privacy Policy when the website, its tools, or its services change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Dev Tool Kit, please contact us through our Contact page.
